Typical Hurghada city tours combine vehicle transfer across parts of the city with stops to walk around local areas, view waterfront and mosque/harbour landmarks, browse local markets or shops if scheduled, and take photos according to the confirmed route and traffic conditions.

  • City Route & Landmarks: Pass through selected districts and waterfront areas in Hurghada.

  • Optional Local Stops: Any market, harbour, or refreshment stop depends on the selected programme.
