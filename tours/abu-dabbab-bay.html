Red Sea Bay & Marine Habitat · Near Marsa Alam

Abu Dabbab Bay

Visit Abu Dabbab Bay near Marsa Alam, known for its sandy shore and seagrass habitat. Sea turtles and dugongs may be present, but wildlife sightings are never guaranteed.

Trip Overview & General Experience Program

Location: Abu Dabbab Bay (Near Marsa Alam) Habitat: Sandy Shore & Seagrass Bay Rate: Confirmed upon booking inquiry

Abu Dabbab Bay is a well-known Red Sea coastal bay with a sandy shore and seagrass areas where marine wildlife such as sea turtles and dugongs may occasionally be spotted in the wild, alongside snorkeling in permitted areas when sea conditions allow.

  • Explore a Red Sea bay and its sandy coastal scenery.
  • Snorkeling in permitted areas, subject to sea conditions.
  • Respectful wildlife watching without touching or disturbing animals.
Important Note: Wildlife sightings (including sea turtles or dugongs) cannot be promised or guaranteed. Always follow local guides, crew instructions, and protected-area / wildlife rules.

Why Visit Abu Dabbab Bay?

Unlike crowded offshore reef stops, Abu Dabbab combines a sandy bay setting with seagrass beds and coastal waters that attract marine life in a natural habitat.

Guests should keep a respectful distance from any marine animals and never touch, chase, or block their path.

“A sandy Red Sea bay and seagrass habitat where nature sets the pace—requiring patience, respect for wildlife, and safe sea conditions.”

Abu Dabbab Bay – Frequently Asked Questions

Are sea turtles or dugongs guaranteed at Abu Dabbab Bay?

No, wildlife sightings cannot be promised or guaranteed because marine animals move freely in their natural habitat.

What rules apply when snorkeling around marine life?

Always follow local guides and protected-area rules, keep a safe distance, and never touch, chase, or disturb marine animals.
