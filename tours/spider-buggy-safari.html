Drive desert tracks in an open-style spider buggy after a vehicle and safety briefing. The vehicle, route, passenger setup and stops depend on the selected package.

Trip Overview & General Experience Program

Category: Desert Safari Vehicle: Open-Style Spider Buggy Rate: Confirmed upon booking inquiry

Typical Hurghada spider buggy excursions transfer guests from the hotel to the desert safari station, begin with a safety and handling briefing, and follow a guided off-road trail across open desert terrain with photo stops and any combined camp or sunset stops included in the selected programme.

  • Transfer & Station Arrival: Pickup/transfer arrangement to the desert starting point according to the confirmed package.
  • Buggy & Safety Briefing: Introduction to the spider buggy controls, seating/harness basics, and desert riding rules.
  • Guided Desert Drive: Drive along an approved desert trail taking in mountain and open-desert views.
  • Photo & Route Stops: Pause at permitted stops along the route, plus any extra activities if part of the selected package.

What to Check Before Departure

  • Driver age/eligibility and passenger seating setup
  • Exact route duration and included transfers
  • Any combined Bedouin stop, tea, or sunset option
  • Dust protection (scarf/keffiyeh, sunglasses, closed shoes)
Important Note: Driver and passenger requirements, vehicle type, seating/passenger setup, route, and stopping points vary by provider. Confirm the exact arrangement before booking.

Off-Road Desert Handling

Open-frame spider buggies offer a lively off-road feel on sandy and gravel desert tracks around Hurghada when driven at a controlled pace behind the guide.

Always keep hands on the wheel, follow spacing rules, and listen to safety instructions at every stop.
