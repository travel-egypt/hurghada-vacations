Desert Tracks & Safety First

Riding in open desert terrain around Hurghada combines sandy and rocky trails with mountain views, while requiring steady group pacing and clear attention to guide instructions.

Bring sunglasses, a scarf/head covering for dust, closed comfortable footwear, and sun protection.
