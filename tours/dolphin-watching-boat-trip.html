Red Sea Boat Excursion · Hurghada

Dolphin Watching Boat Trip

Head out onto the Red Sea by boat to watch for wild dolphins in their natural habitat. Sightings are never guaranteed, and all marine wildlife guidelines apply.

Trip Overview & General Excursion Program

Category: Red Sea Boat Excursion Typical Duration: ~3–4h Speedboat or ~7–8h Day Boat (Package Dependent) Wildlife: Wild Marine Life (Sightings Not Guaranteed) Rate: Confirmed upon booking inquiry

Dolphin watching boat trips from Hurghada typically start with a transfer to the marina and a boat ride into Red Sea waters where dolphins may be encountered in the wild, alongside any scheduled stops, refreshments, or lunch included in the confirmed programme.

  • Hotel Pickup & Marina Boarding: Transfer from your hotel to the departure point and board the boat per your confirmed schedule.
  • Red Sea Cruise & Wildlife Watch: Cruise through open or coastal waters while keeping watch for dolphins and following crew guidance.
  • Programme-Selected Stops: Any water stop, reef stop, lunch, or refreshments depends on the confirmed itinerary and sea conditions.
  • Return Transfer: Return to the marina and transfer back to your hotel.

What to Confirm Before Booking

  • Boat format (speedboat vs. day boat), duration, and hotel pickup time
  • Whether any water/reef stops, meals, or drinks are included
  • Respectful distance rules—never chase, block, or touch marine animals
  • Sun protection, hat, sunglasses, and comfortable boatwear
Important Note: Because dolphins are wild marine animals, sightings can never be guaranteed. Routes, stops, schedules, and water activities are subject to sea and weather conditions and crew safety instructions.

Respecting Wild Marine Life

Seeing wild animals at sea is always subject to nature and sea conditions, so keeping a calm, respectful distance and following the crew’s instructions protects both guests and marine wildlife.

Confirm ahead of time whether your trip is a shorter boat cruise or a longer programme with scheduled stops.

Dolphin Watching Boat Trip – Frequently Asked Questions

Are dolphin sightings guaranteed during the boat trip?

No, dolphins live freely in the wild, so sightings depend on natural movement and sea conditions and cannot be guaranteed.

What rules apply around dolphins and marine wildlife?

Always follow the boat crew and local guidelines, keep a respectful distance, and never chase, touch, feed, or block marine animals.
