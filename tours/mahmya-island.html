Giftun Island National Park – Mahmya Beach

Enjoy a full-day boat trip to Mahmya in Giftun Island National Park, with two reef stops and time on the beach. Lunch is served on board, drinks are unlimited, and pickup is from inside your hotel.

Trip Overview & Typical Island Day

Pickup: Before 09:00 AM (Inside Your Hotel) Departure: 09:00 AM from Hurghada Marina Return: Around 04:00 PM to Marina Rate: Confirmed upon booking inquiry

Sail across the Red Sea toward the Giftun Islands for a relaxed full day combining coral-reef snorkeling stops, time on Mahmya beach, lunch served on board, and unlimited drinks throughout the trip.

Your Day at a Glance

  1. Before 09:00 AM – Hotel Pickup: Our driver collects you from inside your hotel and takes you directly to Hurghada Marina.
  2. 09:00 AM – Boat Departure: Meet the crew, board the boat and cruise towards the Giftun Islands.
  3. First Snorkeling Stop: Explore a selected coral-reef area and look for colourful Red Sea fish with guidance from the crew.
  4. Mahmya Beach Time: Enjoy the beach, turquoise water, swimming and time to relax on the island. Beach access and facilities follow the selected Mahmya arrangement.
  5. Lunch on Board: Return to the main boat for lunch. Unlimited drinks are available throughout the trip.
  6. Second Snorkeling Stop: Enjoy another opportunity to explore the reef and underwater scenery, subject to the captain’s route and sea conditions.
  7. Around 04:00 PM – Return to Marina: Cruise back to Hurghada Marina; arrival time is approximate and may vary.
  8. Hotel Drop-off: Your transfer takes you back to the same hotel where pickup began.

What is Included

  • Pickup from inside your hotel and return to the same hotel
  • Air-conditioned transfer to and from Hurghada Marina
  • Full-day boat trip
  • Two snorkeling stops
  • Snorkeling equipment
  • Lunch served on board
  • Unlimited drinks throughout the trip
Important Note: Hotel pickup, lunch on board and unlimited drinks are included in this programme. Beach access and facilities follow the selected Mahmya arrangement; route and timing may vary with sea and weather conditions.

Mahmya Beach – Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Mahmya located?

Mahmya is on Giftun Island National Park in the Red Sea off the coast of Hurghada, Egypt.

What time does hotel pickup and boat departure start?

Hotel pickup takes place before 09:00 AM with transfer to Hurghada Marina, boat departure around 09:00 AM, and return to the marina around 04:00 PM (approximate timing).

Are lunch, drinks, and snorkeling equipment included?

Yes, hotel pickup and return, air-conditioned marina transfers, two snorkeling stops, snorkeling equipment, lunch on board, and unlimited drinks throughout the trip are included in this programme.
