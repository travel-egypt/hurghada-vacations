A Giftun Island Escape · Hurghada, Egypt

Orange Bay Island Boat Trip

Sail out to the Giftun Islands and spend a full day swimming at Orange Bay’s white sandy beach and shallow turquoise lagoon on comfortable boats, with breakfast and lunch included.

Trip Overview & Typical Island Day

Duration: 7–8 Hours (Full Day) Departure: Hurghada Marina Reference Rate: From €26.44 / $28 (confirm current Atlantis rate)

Sail out to Mahmya and Giftun / Orange Bay’s white sandy beaches and clear turquoise water on comfortable boats, with morning hotel pickup, breakfast and lunch included, and return before sunset.

Your Day at a Glance

  1. Morning – Hotel Pickup: Collect you from inside your hotel and transfer directly to Hurghada Marina.
  2. 09:00 AM – Board & Sail: Board the boat at Hurghada Marina and cruise across the Red Sea toward Giftun & Orange Bay.
  3. First Snorkeling Stop: Snorkel and swim over Red Sea coral reefs.
  4. Orange Bay Island: Enjoy the white sandy beach, shallow turquoise lagoon, swimming, and relaxation.
  5. Breakfast & Lunch on Board: Breakfast and lunch served on board, with unlimited drinks throughout the trip.
  6. Second Snorkeling Stop: Additional snorkeling stop at a reef selected by the crew.
  7. Around 04:00 PM – Return to Marina: Cruise back to Hurghada Marina before sunset.
  8. Before Sunset – Hotel Drop-off: Return to your hotel.

What is Included

  • Morning hotel pickup and return drop-off before sunset
  • Transfer to and from Hurghada Marina
  • Full-day boat trip (approx. 7–8 hours)
  • Breakfast and lunch on board
  • Unlimited drinks throughout the trip
  • Snorkeling stops and equipment (per programme)

More Than a Beautiful Photograph

Orange Bay is more than a beautiful photograph. It is a day made of little moments that become lasting memories. Walk along the pier, look into the clear water, feel the warm sand, and take in the open Red Sea around you.

Find a quiet spot to relax, enjoy the sunshine, or step into the water where swimming is permitted and sea conditions are suitable.

“Sometimes the best journeys are not about going far away. They are about finding a place where you can slow down, breathe deeply and enjoy being there.”

Beneath the Surface

The waters around Giftun Island are part of the remarkable Red Sea marine environment. Snorkeling stops offer a chance to discover coral formations and colourful fish while following crew safety instructions and local reef-protection guidance.

A Day for Everyone

Ideal for families, friends, couples, or solo travellers looking to unwind by the water, capture memorable photos, and enjoy an easy island escape from Hurghada.

Orange Bay – Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Orange Bay located?

Orange Bay is a beach destination on Giftun Island, off the coast of Hurghada in the Red Sea, Egypt.

How do visitors get to Orange Bay?

Guests are picked up from their hotel in the morning, transferred to Hurghada Marina, and sail by boat to the Giftun / Orange Bay area, returning before sunset.

Are meals and drinks included?

Yes, breakfast and lunch on board along with unlimited drinks throughout the trip are included in the programme.

What should I bring?

Bring swimwear, a towel, sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat, and a camera or phone for photos.
