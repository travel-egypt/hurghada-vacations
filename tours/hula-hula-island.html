Hula Hula Island

Enjoy Hula Hula’s lively beach-club atmosphere, music and time beside the Red Sea. Any snorkeling stops and the order of activities follow the confirmed daily programme.

Trip Overview & Typical Island Day

Pickup: Before 09:00 AM (Inside Your Hotel) Departure: 09:00 AM from Hurghada Marina Return: Around 04:00 PM to Marina Rate: Confirmed upon booking inquiry

We collect you from inside your hotel, take you directly to the marina and boat, then return you to the same hotel after the trip. Your exact pickup time is confirmed according to your hotel location.

Your Day at a Glance

  1. Before 09:00 AM – Hotel Pickup: Your day begins with pickup from inside your hotel and a comfortable transfer to the marina.
  2. 09:00 AM – Boat Departure: Board the boat and cruise towards the Hula Hula beach destination.
  3. First Snorkeling Stop: Explore a selected Red Sea reef and enjoy the colourful underwater life.
  4. Hula Hula Island – Beach Club Atmosphere: Enjoy the beach, swim in clear water, relax, listen to the atmosphere and take photos. Any organised entertainment depends on the day’s programme.
  5. Lunch on Board: Return to the boat for lunch, with unlimited drinks available throughout the trip.
  6. Second Snorkeling Stop: Enjoy a second reef stop before the return journey, subject to sea conditions.
  7. Around 04:00 PM – Return to Marina: Cruise back to Hurghada Marina. The return time is approximate.
  8. Hotel Drop-off: Our transfer takes you back to the same hotel where the day started.

What is Included

  • Pickup from inside your hotel and return to the same hotel
  • Air-conditioned transfer to and from Hurghada Marina
  • Full-day boat trip
  • Two snorkeling stops
  • Snorkeling equipment
  • Lunch served on board
  • Unlimited drinks throughout the trip
  • Crew assistance during the experience
Important Note: Hotel pickup, lunch on board, unlimited drinks and two snorkeling stops are included in this programme. Entertainment and beach facilities depend on the day’s operation, and exact timing may change with sea and weather conditions.

Experience Highlights

  • Boat trip and views of the Red Sea coastline.
  • Time to enjoy the beach environment.
  • Swimming opportunities in suitable areas.
  • Relaxing, photography and enjoying the island atmosphere.
  • Optional water or snorkeling activities if offered in the selected package.
