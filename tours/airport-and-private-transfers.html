Transfer Overview & General Operating Program

Category: Airport & Point-to-Point Transfers Routes: Hurghada Airport, Hotels & Red Sea / Regional Stops Options: Private Car, Van/Minibus or Confirmed Arrangement Rate: Confirmed upon booking inquiry

Transfer services in Hurghada and the Red Sea area cover airport arrivals and departures, hotel-to-hotel or resort transfers, and point-to-point road transport based on pickup address, drop-off location, passenger count, luggage volume, and confirmed timing.

  • Route & Schedule Confirmation: Share pickup point, drop-off destination, passenger count, luggage needs, and flight or departure details in advance.
  • Pickup & Meeting Point: Meet at the agreed airport meeting point or hotel entrance/pickup spot according to your confirmed schedule.
  • Road Transfer: Travel along the route to your destination, allowing buffer time for traffic, airport procedures, or road conditions.
  • Drop-Off: Arrive at your hotel, airport terminal, or agreed drop-off point.

What to Confirm Before Booking

  • Exact pickup and drop-off locations (hotel/resort name or airport/route)
  • Flight number, arrival/departure time, or required pickup time
  • Number of passengers, luggage pieces, and vehicle size needed
  • One-way or round-trip option, waiting rules, and confirmed rate
Important Note: Travel times, pickup schedules, and arrival windows depend on traffic, road conditions, airport/security checkpoints, flight delays or schedule changes, and confirmed booking details.
