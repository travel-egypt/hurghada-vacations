Eden Island

Spend the day at Eden Island with two snorkeling stops and a taxi-boat transfer from the main vessel. Enjoy beach time, lunch on board and unlimited drinks, with pickup from inside your hotel.

Trip Overview & Typical Island Day

Pickup: Before 09:00 AM (Inside Your Hotel) Departure: 09:00 AM from Hurghada Marina Return: Around 04:00 PM to Marina Rate: Confirmed upon booking inquiry

We collect you from inside your hotel, take you directly to the marina and boat, then return you to the same hotel after the trip. Your exact pickup time is confirmed according to your hotel location.

Your Day at a Glance

  1. Before 09:00 AM – Hotel Pickup: We collect you from inside your hotel and take you directly to Hurghada Marina.
  2. 09:00 AM – Boat Departure: Board the main boat and enjoy the cruise across the Red Sea.
  3. First Snorkeling Stop: Explore a beautiful reef and discover colourful fish and coral formations.
  4. Taxi Boat to Eden Island: When we reach the island area, transfer from the main boat to a smaller taxi boat that takes you to Eden Island.
  5. Eden Island – Beach & Relaxation: Enjoy free time on the beach: swim, relax, walk along the shore and take memorable photos.
  6. Lunch on the Main Boat: Return to the main boat for lunch. Unlimited drinks are available throughout the trip.
  7. Second Snorkeling Stop: Enjoy another snorkeling experience at a reef location selected by the captain.
  8. Around 04:00 PM – Return to Marina: Cruise back to Hurghada Marina. The return time is approximate and may change with sea conditions.
  9. Hotel Drop-off: Our transfer takes you back to the same hotel where we collected you.
