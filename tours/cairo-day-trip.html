Full-day trips from Hurghada to Cairo typically begin with an early pickup from your hotel and travel to Cairo, covering the Giza Pyramids plateau and selected museum or city stops depending on the confirmed programme, along with planned rest or lunch stops where included.

  • Early Hotel Pickup & Transfer: Depart Hurghada early for the journey to Cairo (exact pickup time confirmed by hotel location and transport option).

  • Return to Hurghada: Head back to Hurghada and return to your hotel later in the day or night.

What to Confirm Before Booking

  • Transport mode (coach/transfer or flight option) and hotel pickup time

  • Whether entrance tickets, guide services, or lunch are included

Important Note: Because Cairo is a long-distance full-day trip from Hurghada, exact schedules, road/traffic conditions, visited sites, entrance fees, transport mode, and included meals must be confirmed before booking.
