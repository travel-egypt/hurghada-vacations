Kitesurfing & Windsurfing

Learn or practice kitesurfing and windsurfing on the Red Sea with sessions adapted to your experience level. Instruction, equipment, launch arrangements, and schedules depend on the centre, wind, and safety conditions.

Session Overview & General Program

Category: Water Sports Activities: Kitesurfing & Windsurfing Conditions: Wind, centre, instructor & safety dependent Rate: Confirmed upon booking inquiry

Whether you are stepping onto a board for the first time or building consistency on the water, sessions are tailored to your level and the day’s wind and sea conditions in approved water areas.

Important Note: Both kitesurfing and windsurfing depend strongly on wind conditions, instructor availability, and participant ability. No specific learning outcome is guaranteed, and sessions may be adjusted or postponed for safety.

Kitesurfing Sessions

  • Introduction to kite equipment and safety systems.
  • Beginner instruction or practice for experienced riders.
  • Understanding wind and water conditions.
  • Use of an approved training or riding area.

Windsurfing Sessions

  • Introduction to the board, sail, and basic techniques.
  • Instruction according to the participant's level.
  • Practice in a suitable water area.
  • Enjoying the sea and wind conditions.
“Real Red Sea wind sessions—whether harnessing a kite or a sail—guided by safety briefings, approved riding zones, and the conditions of the day.”

Kitesurfing & Windsurfing – Frequently Asked Questions

Can I book kitesurfing or windsurfing based on my current level?

Yes, sessions are arranged for beginners starting with equipment basics as well as riders practicing on the water, subject to instructor availability, wind conditions, and participant suitability.

Is a specific progress or learning outcome guaranteed?

No, wind conditions, instructor availability, and individual ability vary, so no specific learning outcome is guaranteed.

What happens if wind or sea conditions are not suitable?

Instruction, equipment, and launch arrangements depend on the centre, wind, and safety conditions, and sessions may be adjusted or postponed for safety.
