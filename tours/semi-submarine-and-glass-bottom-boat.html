Semi-Submarine & Glass Bottom Boat

View Red Sea coral and marine life through underwater viewing windows or glass panels on a coastal excursion suited to families and non-swimmers, subject to sea conditions.

Trip Overview & General Excursion Program

Category: Red Sea Marine Viewing Vessel Type: Semi-Submarine or Glass-Bottom Boat Conditions: Sea & visibility dependent Rate: Confirmed upon booking inquiry

Semi-submarine and glass-bottom boat trips in Hurghada offer a dry, accessible way to look out over coastal waters and reef areas—ideal for guests who prefer not to swim or snorkel, with exact routes, departure times, and any optional stops depending on the selected vessel and programme.

  • Hotel Transfer & Marina Boarding: Arrive at the departure point according to your confirmed transfer schedule and board the vessel.
  • Coastal Cruise & Viewing Area: Head toward approved reef or coastal viewing spots when sea conditions allow.
  • Underwater / Glass Viewing: Observe marine life and reef scenery through viewing windows or glass panels (visibility varies with water and weather conditions).
  • Return Transfer: Return to the marina and head back to your hotel.

What to Confirm Before Booking

  • Whether the vessel is a semi-submarine or glass-bottom boat
  • Departure slot, duration, and hotel transfer arrangement
  • Whether any water/swim stop or refreshments are included
  • Sun protection, sunglasses, hat, and motion-comfort items if needed
Important Note: Underwater visibility, marine life sightings, routes, departure times, vessel availability, and any optional stops depend on sea and weather conditions and the confirmed programme.

Semi-Submarine & Glass Bottom Boat – Frequently Asked Questions

Is marine life or crystal-clear visibility guaranteed on every trip?

No, underwater visibility, sea movement, and marine life sightings depend on natural sea and weather conditions and cannot be guaranteed.

Do I need to know how to swim for this excursion?

No, viewing is done from aboard the semi-submarine or glass-bottom boat, though any optional water or swim stops depend on the specific programme confirmed.
