Trip Overview & General Excursion Program

Category: Cultural & Historical Tour Departure: Early Morning from Hurghada Hotels Format: Full-Day Overland Excursion Rate: Confirmed upon booking inquiry

Full-day excursions from Hurghada to Luxor typically start with an early hotel pickup and overland transfer across the desert to the Nile Valley, covering selected sites on the East and West Banks—such as Karnak Temple, the Valley of the Kings, and historic viewpoints or temples—along with planned rest or lunch stops depending on the confirmed programme.

  • Early Hotel Pickup & Transfer: Depart Hurghada in the morning for the overland journey to Luxor (exact pickup time confirmed by hotel location).
  • Guided Historical Sites: Visit the scheduled Luxor landmarks and archaeological areas included in your confirmed itinerary.
  • Break / Refreshments or Lunch: Take scheduled rest or meal stops according to the selected package.
  • Return Journey to Hurghada: Travel back to Hurghada and return to your hotel in the evening.

What to Confirm Before Booking

  • Exact list of temples or sites included on East & West Banks
  • Whether entrance tickets, guide services, lunch, or drinks are included
  • Estimated departure and evening return times for your hotel
  • Comfortable walking shoes, hat, sunglasses, sun protection, and water
Important Note: Because Luxor is a long full-day trip from Hurghada, exact schedules, road/security/site conditions, visited sites, entrance fees, and included meals must be confirmed before booking.
