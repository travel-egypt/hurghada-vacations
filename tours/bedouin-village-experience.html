Trip Overview & General Experience Program

Category: Desert Safari Setting: Desert Camp / Village Around Hurghada Rate: Confirmed upon booking inquiry

Desert Bedouin village excursions around Hurghada take guests from the coast into the desert interior to experience traditional hospitality, desert surroundings, and optional activities or meals depending on the confirmed itinerary.

  • Desert Transfer: Travel from Hurghada to the desert camp or village location according to the confirmed arrangement.
  • Hospitality & Traditions: Learn about Bedouin customs, desert life, and traditional hospitality (such as tea when included).
  • Optional Desert Activities: Any camel ride, sunset viewpoint, dinner, or evening entertainment depends on the selected programme.
  • Return Transfer: Head back to your hotel after the visit concludes.

What to Confirm Before Booking

  • Exact pickup/return schedule from your hotel
  • Whether tea, dinner, or refreshments are included
  • Whether a camel ride, stargazing, or evening show is part of the package
  • Comfortable clothing, closed shoes, and sun or evening layers
Important Note: Exact activities, meals, drinks, camel rides, entertainment and schedules vary by programme. Always treat local communities and animals respectfully and confirm inclusions before booking.
