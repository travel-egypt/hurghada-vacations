Parasailing

Lift off above Red Sea waters towed by a dedicated parasailing boat after a safety briefing and harness fitting. Flight duration, setup, and timing depend on wind, sea conditions, and your confirmed booking.

Trip Overview & General Experience Program

Category: Red Sea Water Activity Setup: Towed Parasail from Boat Conditions: Wind, sea & weather dependent Rate: Confirmed upon booking inquiry

Parasailing excursions in Hurghada typically arrange hotel transfer to the launch boat or marina, followed by a safety briefing, life vest and harness check, takeoff and tow above the water, landing back on the boat or water as directed by the crew, and return transfer.

  • Transfer & Boat Boarding: Head to the launch point/boat according to your confirmed pickup schedule.
  • Safety Briefing & Harness Check: Receive crew instructions on launch, mid-air posture, landing, and safety rules.
  • Towed Flight Over the Water: Ride towed behind the boat above the Red Sea for the duration set by the operator and conditions.
  • Landing & Return: Return to the boat and transfer back to your hotel.

What to Confirm Before Booking

  • Single or tandem/shared flight setup and weight/age limits
  • Expected time in the air vs. boat transfer/waiting time
  • Whether hotel transfers or photos/videos are included or separate
  • Swimwear or comfortable quick-dry clothes, sun protection, and secure items
Important Note: Parasailing flights, launch/landing procedures, flight time, and schedules depend heavily on wind speed, sea state, weather, and operator safety rules, and may be adjusted or postponed if conditions are unsuitable.

Wind, Sea & Crew Instructions

Unlike land tours, parasailing is directly governed by wind and sea conditions—so listening carefully to the crew and accepting weather-based adjustments is essential for a safe outing.

Leave loose phones, hats, or sunglasses unsecured unless properly fastened per crew guidance.
