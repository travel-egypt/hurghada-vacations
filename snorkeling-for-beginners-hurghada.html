Snorkeling for Beginners in Hurghada

Your first snorkeling adventure does not need to be complicated. Discover the equipment, basic techniques and practical safety advice that can help you prepare for the Red Sea.

Start with Confidence

What Is Snorkeling?

Snorkeling is a surface water activity that allows you to observe underwater life using a mask and a breathing tube called a snorkel.

Unlike scuba diving, snorkeling does not require an air tank or diving equipment. You normally float on the water's surface while looking down at the underwater world.

Hurghada is a popular destination for snorkeling because the Red Sea offers opportunities to explore coral reefs, colorful fish and fascinating marine life.

If you have never snorkeled before, there is no need to rush. Learning the basic equipment and getting comfortable in the water are important first steps.

Is Snorkeling Difficult for Beginners?

Many people find the basic technique straightforward after practicing in calm, shallow water with suitable supervision and properly fitted equipment.

However, snorkeling is still a water activity. Confidence, swimming ability, sea conditions and personal comfort all matter.

Do You Need to Be a Strong Swimmer?

You should tell your tour provider about your swimming ability before entering the water. Non-swimmers should not assume that snorkeling is safe without appropriate supervision and suitable flotation equipment.

Beginner Essentials

  • A properly fitting snorkeling mask
  • A suitable snorkel
  • Fins in the correct size
  • Suitable flotation equipment when needed
  • A supervised and suitable location
  • Knowledge of basic water safety
  • Respect for coral reefs and marine life
Know Your Equipment

Snorkeling Equipment Explained

Understanding the basic equipment helps beginners feel more comfortable before entering the water.

🥽

Snorkeling Mask

The mask allows you to see underwater. It should fit comfortably and create a suitable seal around your face.

🤿

Snorkel Tube

The snorkel lets you breathe at the surface while keeping your face in the water.

🦶

Swimming Fins

Fins help you move through the water more efficiently. They should fit securely without causing pain.

🦺

Life Jacket

Suitable flotation equipment can provide additional support. Ask the crew what is available and appropriate for your needs.

☀️

Sun Protection

Protect exposed skin with suitable sun protection, clothing and a hat when you are out of the water.

👕

Swimwear

Choose comfortable swimwear that allows you to move freely and enjoy your time in the water.

Step by Step

How to Start Snorkeling for the First Time

Take your time and follow the instructions of a qualified guide or crew member.

1

Choose the Right Equipment

Make sure the mask, snorkel and fins fit properly. Ask the crew for help adjusting your equipment before entering the water.

2

Listen to the Safety Briefing

Learn where you can enter the water, which areas are suitable, where the crew will be and what signals to use if you need assistance.

3

Start in a Suitable Calm Area

Beginners should start in a suitable supervised area with manageable conditions. Never enter the sea if you feel unsafe.

4

Practice Breathing Through the Snorkel

Keep your breathing calm and steady. Do not force yourself to continue if you feel uncomfortable or anxious.

5

Float and Move Slowly

Keep your movements relaxed and avoid sudden kicks. Do not touch the seabed or coral reefs.

6

Take Breaks Whenever Needed

Return to the boat or designated safe area if you become tired, cold, anxious or uncomfortable.

Safety First

Important Snorkeling Safety Tips

A safe experience starts before you enter the water. Never underestimate the sea.

👥

Never Snorkel Alone

Stay with your buddy or supervised group. Follow the crew's instructions and remain within designated areas.

🌬️

Check Sea Conditions

Wind, currents and waves can make snorkeling difficult. Follow the crew's decision about water entry.

🦺

Use Suitable Flotation

Ask for appropriate flotation equipment if you need additional support. Wear it as instructed.

🪸

Protect the Coral

Never stand on, touch or break coral. Keep a safe distance from marine animals and reef structures.

💧

Stay Hydrated

Drink enough water throughout the day. Avoid entering the water when you feel unwell or exhausted.

🛟

Know When to Stop

Stop immediately if you experience breathing difficulties, panic, dizziness or unusual fatigue. Signal the crew for assistance.

Important Reminder

Snorkeling is not suitable for everyone in every condition. If you cannot swim, have a medical concern or feel uncomfortable in the water, discuss this with your provider before participating. Never rely on a life jacket as a substitute for supervision or basic water safety.

Families and Children

Is Snorkeling Suitable for Families?

Snorkeling can be an enjoyable family activity when the location, equipment and supervision are suitable for everyone.

Children should always be supervised closely by a responsible adult. Age limits, equipment requirements and water activities vary between tour operators.

Tips for Parents

Choose properly fitting equipment. Make sure children understand the safety briefing and know how to communicate with the supervising adult.

Never force a child to enter the water. Some children may prefer to stay on the boat and enjoy the sea views.

Ask your provider about child-specific flotation equipment, supervision, minimum ages and suitable locations before booking.

Frequently Asked Questions

Snorkeling for Beginners FAQ

Can beginners go snorkeling in Hurghada?

Yes, beginners can participate in suitable snorkeling activities when conditions, supervision and equipment are appropriate. Always follow the crew's safety instructions.

Do I need to know how to swim to snorkel?

You should tell your provider if you cannot swim. Do not assume snorkeling is safe for non-swimmers. Suitable flotation, supervision and a suitable location are essential considerations.

Is snorkeling safe for first-time visitors?

It can be an enjoyable activity when properly planned and supervised. Sea conditions, equipment, health and swimming ability all affect safety.

How do I breathe through a snorkel?

A snorkel is designed for breathing while your face is in the water at the surface. Practice calmly in a suitable supervised area and follow your instructor's guidance.

Can children go snorkeling?

Some trips may accept children, depending on age, swimming ability, equipment and safety arrangements. Check the provider's requirements before booking.

Do I need to buy my own snorkeling equipment?

Some boat excursions provide equipment, while others may have different arrangements. Confirm whether a mask, snorkel and fins are included.

Can I wear glasses while snorkeling?

Ordinary glasses are generally not designed to be worn under a snorkeling mask. Ask about prescription masks or suitable alternatives if you need vision correction.

What if I feel afraid in the water?

Do not force yourself to continue. Inform the crew, return to a safe designated area and take a break. You can enjoy the boat trip without entering the water.

Can I touch the coral or fish?

No. Observe marine life without touching or disturbing it. Avoid standing on coral and never feed wild fish.

How do I choose a beginner-friendly snorkeling trip?

Ask about the planned locations, group supervision, equipment, flotation options, safety briefing and whether the itinerary is suitable for first-time visitors.
