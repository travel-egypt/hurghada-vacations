GETTING AROUND

Taxis & Local Transport

A few simple habits can make getting around Hurghada easier.

🚕

Before taking a taxi

Agree clearly before the ride
✓
Confirm the total fare

Agree on the full price and currency before getting in, unless the meter is being used.

✓
Clarify the destination

Show the hotel or destination name on your phone, and check the route before departure.

✓
Keep small notes

Carry some smaller Egyptian-pound notes for everyday purchases and transport.

💳

Money & Shopping

Simple ways to avoid confusion
✓
Check the currency

Confirm whether a quoted price is in Egyptian pounds, euros or another currency.

✓
Agree before buying

For market goods and services without displayed prices, agree on the price before accepting.

✓
Use trusted exchange services

Exchange money through banks or licensed exchange offices and keep your receipt.

We intentionally do not publish fixed taxi or shopping prices here because fares and prices change. Confirm the current amount before you agree.

STAY CONNECTED

SIM Cards & Mobile Internet

A local SIM can be useful for maps, messaging and contacting your hotel or tour operator.

📶

Getting a local SIM

Choose an official provider
1
Visit an official operator counter

Look for an official mobile-network shop or airport counter rather than an unverified street seller.

2
Bring your passport

Ask the provider which identification documents are required to register and activate your SIM.

3
Check the package details

Confirm the data allowance, validity period, local minutes and total price before paying.

4
Test before leaving

Make sure mobile data works and keep the receipt and provider details.

📱

Before you use your phone

Avoid unexpected costs
✓
Check roaming charges

If you keep your home SIM active, check international roaming rates with your home provider.

✓
Download offline maps

Save the Hurghada area in your map app before leaving Wi-Fi.

✓
Keep important details

Save your hotel address, travel insurance details and tour contact information offline.

Enjoy your time in Hurghada while keeping the essentials in mind.

Sun & Beach

Take care in the Egyptian sun
✓
Stay hydrated

Carry drinking water, especially during outdoor activities and boat trips.

✓
Use sun protection

Wear suitable sun protection, sunglasses and a hat. Take breaks in the shade.

✓
Respect the reef

Do not stand on, touch or collect coral. Follow your guide's instructions in the water.
