Smart travel tips

A few useful things to know before you head out.

📱 SIM cards & Internet

  • Ask about the data allowance and validity period before purchasing.
  • Keep your passport available when buying a local SIM.
  • Save your hotel address offline.

☀️ Sun, heat & beach

  • Drink water regularly, especially during outdoor activities.
  • Use sunscreen, sunglasses and a hat.
  • Follow local beach and sea safety instructions.

💵 Money & shopping

  • Ask for the price before agreeing to buy or use a service.
  • Carry some small Egyptian pound notes.
  • Check your change before leaving.

🤝 Local culture

  • A friendly greeting and a smile go a long way.
  • Ask permission before photographing people.
  • Dress appropriately when visiting religious places.
