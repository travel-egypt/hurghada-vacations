KNOW BEFORE YOU GO

Understanding the Sea Report

A few useful details to help you read the marine forecast.

🌊 What does wave height mean?

Wave height is the forecast significant wave height. It describes the average height of the highest one-third of waves, not the maximum individual wave.

💨 Why does wind matter?

Wind can influence surface conditions, boat comfort and the way the sea changes during the day. Gusts may be stronger than the average wind speed.

🌡️ What is sea temperature?

Sea surface temperature is a model estimate for the water near the surface. It can differ from the temperature at your swimming or diving location.

🧭 What is wave direction?

Direction is shown in degrees and describes where waves are coming from. Local reefs and coastlines can change conditions at individual sites.
