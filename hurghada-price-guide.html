Choose with confidence

What Should a Fair Excursion Price Include?

Two trips can have different prices because the details are different. Compare the full experience, not just the headline price.

🚐

Hotel Transfers

Check whether pickup and drop-off are included, and whether remote hotels have an extra transfer charge.

🚤

Boat & Island Access

Ask which boat, island entry or beach access fees are included in the quoted price.

🍽️

Food & Drinks

Confirm whether lunch, water, soft drinks or other refreshments are part of the package.

🤿

Equipment

Check whether snorkeling masks, fins, life jackets or other equipment are provided.

👨‍✈️

Guides & Support

Ask whether a guide or trip coordinator is available and what support is provided during the trip.

💳

Extra Charges

Clarify taxes, optional activities, photo packages, tips and any additional fees before confirming.

Smart spending tips

How to Avoid Paying More Than Expected

Simple habits that help visitors understand prices and make better choices in Hurghada.

🚕
Agree on taxi fares first

Confirm the full fare, destination and currency before starting your journey. Ask whether the price is per car or per person.

📋
Ask for the complete price

For restaurants, activities and services, check whether taxes, service charges or extras are included.

🏷️
Check displayed prices

Supermarkets and established stores usually display item prices. Compare the size, brand and unit before buying.

💱
Confirm the currency

Always check whether a quote is in Egyptian Pounds, euros or US dollars. Never assume.

📸
Keep a written confirmation

For a tour or private transfer, save the agreed price and the list of included services in a message.

🤝
Compare like with like

A cheaper trip may exclude transfers, island entry, lunch or equipment. Compare the same inclusions.

Frequently asked questions

Hurghada Prices – FAQs

Are these official prices in Hurghada?

No. The everyday prices are indicative community-reported references, not official government tariffs or guaranteed shop prices. Individual businesses may charge different amounts.

Are prices shown per person or per item?

Each price card specifies its unit, such as per meal, per kilogram, per litre, per ticket or per month. Always check the unit before comparing prices.

Why can the same product have different prices?

Brand, quality, shop type, imported or locally produced goods, location and seasonal changes can all affect the price.

How can I check the current price of an Atlantis excursion?

Choose the experience you are interested in and contact Atlantis Tours and Experiences to confirm the current offer, availability, inclusions and final price.

Does a boat trip price always include lunch and drinks?

No. Inclusions vary between operators and packages. Ask for a written list of what is included before booking.

How often should I check this guide?

Use it as a planning reference and confirm important prices locally before paying. Market prices and exchange rates can change.
