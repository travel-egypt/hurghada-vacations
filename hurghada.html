A story by the sea

From a fishing shore to a world of discovery

Before the promenades, resorts and boats filled with visitors, Hurghada belonged to the rhythm of the sea.

Its story is rooted in the lives of fishermen who watched the changing water, read the wind and knew the coastline by heart. The settlement grew around the shore and its fishing harbour. Over time, travellers discovered what local people had always known: the Red Sea here is a world of extraordinary colour, life and light.

As the city expanded, new neighbourhoods, hotels, marinas and diving centres appeared along the coast. Yet Hurghada is more than a holiday strip. In El Dahar, everyday Egyptian life continues in markets, bakeries and cafés. In Sakkala, the city opens towards the harbour and its lively waterfront. Further south, the promenades and resorts meet the long horizon of the sea.

To understand Hurghada, look beyond the beach. Walk through its old streets, pause beneath the minarets of El Mina Mosque, visit the museum, watch the boats return to the marina and leave time for the islands and reefs offshore. The city reveals itself in layers—local, modern, maritime and welcoming.

Hurghada is not only a place to stay beside the Red Sea. It is a place to discover the many stories that the sea has brought to its shore.

A few useful notes

Make the most of your visit

When to explore

For walking around town, mornings and late afternoons are usually more comfortable than the strongest midday heat. Check seasonal conditions before planning outdoor visits.

Getting around

Taxis and local minibuses connect the main districts. Agree the fare before starting a taxi journey, or use a trusted ride-hailing option where available.

Respect local places

Dress modestly when visiting religious sites, ask before photographing people, and follow staff or guide instructions at museums and attractions.

Beyond the city

The Red Sea is waiting offshore

Some of Hurghada's best-known experiences are reached by boat and are not inside the city itself. Giftun Island, Orange Bay and Mahmya are island excursions; routes and access depend on the operator, permits and sea conditions.
