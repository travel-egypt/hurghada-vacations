Your Red Sea Guide

Why Go Snorkeling in Hurghada?

Hurghada is one of Egypt's best-known destinations for enjoying the Red Sea. Its coastal waters offer opportunities to discover coral formations and observe marine life in a beautiful natural setting.

If you are planning a holiday in Egypt, snorkeling in Hurghada is an experience worth considering. The Red Sea is famous for its underwater landscapes, clear-water locations and rich variety of marine life.

Snorkeling allows you to explore the underwater world while floating on the surface with a mask and snorkel. You do not need scuba-diving certification to enjoy a suitable beginner-friendly snorkeling experience.

Many visitors choose to enjoy snorkeling as part of a boat trip. Depending on the trip and sea conditions, the day may include time at selected reef locations, swimming breaks and relaxing on board.

A Different Side of Your Hurghada Holiday

A day on the Red Sea offers a change of scenery from the hotel, city and desert. You can enjoy the sea breeze, discover underwater scenery and spend time surrounded by the natural beauty of Egypt's coastline.

Whether you are traveling as a couple, with friends or with family, choose an excursion that matches your interests, swimming ability and comfort level.

Choose Your Experience

Popular Ways to Enjoy the Red Sea

There are different ways to enjoy the sea around Hurghada. The right choice depends on the kind of holiday experience you are looking for.

Snorkeling Boat Trips

A snorkeling boat trip gives you the opportunity to travel from the coast to selected locations on the Red Sea. Depending on the itinerary, the experience may include snorkeling stops, time to swim and relaxation on board.

Island and Beach Trips

Some sea excursions combine a boat journey with time on an island or beach. These trips can be a good option for visitors who want a mixture of sea views, relaxation and swimming.

Private Sea Experiences

Travelers who prefer a more personal experience can ask about private boat options, where available. Before booking, confirm the route, duration, inclusions and arrangements directly with the provider.

Snorkeling for Beginners

First-time snorkelers can start with calm conditions, suitable equipment and guidance from a qualified trip crew. If you are not confident in the water, tell your guide before entering and use appropriate flotation support.

Before You Go

Snorkeling Tips for a Better Experience

A little preparation can make your day on the Red Sea more comfortable, enjoyable and safe.

1

Choose the Right Equipment

Use a properly fitting mask and snorkel. Ask the crew for help if your equipment feels uncomfortable or leaks.

2

Protect Yourself from the Sun

Bring suitable sun protection, sunglasses, a hat and light clothing for time spent on the boat.

3

Stay Hydrated

Drink enough water throughout the day, especially during hot weather and outdoor activities.

4

Listen to Your Guide

Follow the crew's instructions about entry points, swimming areas, currents and changing sea conditions.

5

Respect Marine Life

Never touch, stand on or collect coral. Keep a respectful distance from fish and other marine animals.

6

Be Honest About Your Swimming Ability

Tell the crew if you are a beginner or cannot swim confidently. Never enter the water beyond your ability.

Protect the Red Sea

Enjoy Snorkeling Responsibly

Coral reefs are living ecosystems. They provide shelter for many marine species and are an important part of the Red Sea's natural environment.

Help protect this underwater world by keeping your distance from coral, avoiding contact with marine animals and taking all rubbish back to shore. Do not feed fish or remove anything from the sea.

Responsible snorkeling helps preserve the beauty of the Red Sea for future visitors and for the wildlife that depends on it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Snorkeling in Hurghada FAQ

Useful answers to common questions before planning your Red Sea snorkeling experience.

Is Hurghada good for snorkeling?

Hurghada is a popular destination for snorkeling because of its access to the Red Sea and nearby reef locations. Conditions and visibility vary depending on the day and location.

Do I need experience to go snorkeling?

No previous snorkeling experience is necessary for a suitable beginner-friendly trip. You should be comfortable with the water, use appropriate equipment and follow the crew's instructions.

Can non-swimmers join a snorkeling boat trip?

Non-swimmers may be able to join the boat excursion, but entering the water requires appropriate safety arrangements and supervision. Tell the provider about your swimming ability before booking and never enter the water unless it is safe for you.

What should I bring for a snorkeling trip?

Bring swimwear, a towel, sun protection, sunglasses, a hat and drinking water. Check with your trip provider about which equipment is included.

Can children go snorkeling in Hurghada?

Some excursions may be suitable for children, depending on their age, swimming ability, weather and sea conditions. Always check age requirements and safety arrangements with the provider before booking.

When is the best time to go snorkeling?

Snorkeling is possible during much of the year in Hurghada. The best conditions for your trip depend on wind, waves, water temperature and visibility on the chosen date.

Will I definitely see dolphins or turtles?

No wildlife sighting can be guaranteed. Dolphins, turtles and other marine animals live in their natural environment, and sightings depend on nature and the conditions of the day.
