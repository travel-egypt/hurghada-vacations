The Island Story

A Journey to a Different World

Imagine leaving the busy streets of Hurghada behind. The sound of the city slowly fades as your boat glides across the beautiful Red Sea. The breeze touches your face, sunlight dances across the water, and somewhere ahead a bright sandy shoreline is waiting for you.

Welcome to Orange Bay, a well-known beach destination on Giftun Island, off the coast of Hurghada. Here, the sea shifts between beautiful shades of blue and turquoise, while the shoreline invites you to slow down, breathe deeply and enjoy the moment.

More Than a View

More Than a Beautiful Photograph

Orange Bay is more than a beautiful photograph. It is a day made of little moments that become lasting memories. Walk along the pier, look into the clear water, feel the warm sand and take in the open sea around you.

Find a quiet place to relax, enjoy the sunshine or step into the water where swimming is permitted and conditions are suitable. The simple pleasure of being beside the Red Sea is what makes an island day feel special.

“Sometimes the best journeys are not about going far away. They are about finding a place where you can slow down, breathe deeply and enjoy being there.”
Beneath the Surface

Discover the Underwater World

The waters around Giftun Island are part of the remarkable Red Sea marine environment. Many boat excursions include snorkeling stops, offering guests a chance to discover coral formations and observe marine life from the water's surface.

Whether it is your first snorkeling experience or one of many, take your time, use the appropriate equipment and follow the crew's safety instructions and local reef-protection guidance.

Made for Shared Memories

A Day for Everyone

Come with family and enjoy a day together. Come with friends and fill the hours with laughter. Come as a couple and enjoy the sea views, or simply give yourself a little time away from everyday routines.

Take photographs, enjoy the beach, explore the water and let the rhythm of the island replace the rush of daily life. The best part is often the feeling of having nowhere else you need to be.

Orange Bay – Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Orange Bay located?

Orange Bay is a popular beach destination on Giftun Island, off the coast of Hurghada in the Red Sea, Egypt.

How do visitors get to Orange Bay?

Visitors usually reach the island by boat from Hurghada as part of an organised sea excursion.

Can I go snorkeling during the trip?

Many boat excursions include snorkeling stops. The exact programme and locations depend on the selected trip, local rules and sea conditions.

Is Orange Bay suitable for families?

It can be an enjoyable day out for families. Children must always be supervised near the water and everyone should follow the crew's safety guidance.

What should I bring?

Consider bringing swimwear, a towel, sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat and a camera or phone for photographs. Check any trip-specific advice before departure.

Is the trip programme always the same?

No. Boat schedules, activities, snorkeling stops and time on the island can vary according to the selected excursion, weather, sea conditions and operator arrangements.

Take the Feeling Home With You

And when the time comes to sail back to Hurghada, you may find yourself looking over your shoulder, wishing the day could last a little longer. Orange Bay is not just a place you visit. It is a feeling you take home with you.

Ancient Thebes

Luxor & The Ancient Temples

Discover Luxor, one of Egypt's most important historical destinations. The city preserves extraordinary monuments from ancient Thebes, including vast temple complexes, royal tombs and monumental statues.

Places & Possible Highlights

  • Karnak Temple: an extensive religious complex famous for its monumental columns, pylons and sacred lake.
