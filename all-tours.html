Orange Bay – Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Orange Bay located?

Orange Bay is a popular beach destination on Giftun Island, off the coast of Hurghada in the Red Sea, Egypt.

How do visitors get to Orange Bay?

Visitors usually reach the island by boat from Hurghada as part of an organised sea excursion.

Can I go snorkeling during the trip?

Many boat excursions include snorkeling stops. The exact programme and locations depend on the selected trip, local rules and sea conditions.

Is Orange Bay suitable for families?

It can be an enjoyable day out for families. Children must always be supervised near the water and everyone should follow the crew's safety guidance.

What should I bring?

Consider bringing swimwear, a towel, sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat and a camera or phone for photographs. Check any trip-specific advice before departure.

Is the trip programme always the same?

No. Boat schedules, activities, snorkeling stops and time on the island can vary according to the selected excursion, weather, sea conditions and operator arrangements.
